Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who we are

Balay & Beyond operates this website and is responsible for processing the personal data you provide when you browse, register, or place an order. References to "we", "us", or "our" in this document refer to Balay & Beyond.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account data: name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Order data: delivery address, order history, and purchase amounts.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, search queries on-site, and session duration via server logs. We do not use third-party analytics tracking scripts.
  • Communications: messages you send through our contact form.

3. How we use your information

Your data is used only for the purposes listed below. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders.
  • Sending order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Responding to customer support queries.
  • Improving website performance and detecting errors.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secured servers. Passwords are stored as salted cryptographic hashes and are never readable, even internally. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. No internet transmission is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take it seriously.

5. Cookies

We use session cookies strictly to manage your login state and shopping cart. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so will prevent you from staying logged in or maintaining your cart between visits.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data via your account profile.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our contact page.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the revised date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions about this policy? Contact us and we will respond within two business days.
