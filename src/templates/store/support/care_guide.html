Care Guide

Our products are designed to last, but a little care goes a long way. Follow these guidelines to keep your Balay & Beyond pieces looking beautiful for years to come.

Wooden Furniture & Accents

  • Dust regularly with a soft, dry cloth.
  • Avoid using harsh chemical cleaners or silicone-based polishes.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and extreme temperature changes to prevent warping or fading.
  • Use coasters or felt pads to protect surfaces from heat and moisture.

Textiles & Linens

  • Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle unless otherwise specified.
  • Do not bleach. Tumble dry low or line dry in the shade.
  • Iron on a low setting if necessary.

Ceramics & Glassware

  • Hand washing is recommended for artisanal ceramics to preserve their glaze.
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes to prevent cracking.
  • Microwave and dishwasher safety depends on the specific item—please check the product details.

Need more specific care advice? Contact our team and we'll be happy to help.
