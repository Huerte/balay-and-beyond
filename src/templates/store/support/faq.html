Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

Do you have a physical store?

Currently, Balay & Beyond operates exclusively online to bring you the best prices on our curated collections. We occasionally host pop-up events in Metro Manila.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Orders are processed quickly, but if you contact us within 2 hours of placing your order, we will do our best to accommodate modifications or cancellations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), GCash, and Maya. All transactions are securely processed.

Are your products sustainably sourced?

We work closely with our suppliers to ensure responsible sourcing. Many of our wooden and woven products are handcrafted by local artisans using sustainable materials.

Have another question? Contact us and we will respond within two business days.
