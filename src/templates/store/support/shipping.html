Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about delivery and returns.

Shipping Information

We currently ship across the Philippines. Orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

  • Metro Manila: 2-3 business days (₱150 standard flat rate)
  • Provincial: 5-7 business days (₱300 standard flat rate)

*Free shipping on orders over ₱5,000.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in their original condition and packaging.

To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number. Please note that return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
